# Oragrol Company / About Page — Final Content

## 1. Founder Bio

**Mohammad Chelouy Tabrizi**
Founder & CEO

Mohammad built his first cybersecurity company in Iran in 2012, well before Oragrol existed. Running that business, rather than just studying the industry, is where the practical, business first approach behind Oragrol actually comes from.

In the years since, he has spent more than fifteen years in regional market expansion, business development, and international operations, including building partnerships across Southeast Asia. His academic background includes software engineering, an MBA, and a PhD in Management.

He founded Oragrol Global in Toronto in 2025 to bring that same practical approach to businesses across Canada and North America, starting with cybersecurity and building a broader technology company around it over time.

---

## 2. Company Mission / Story

**Why Oragrol Exists**

Oragrol was built on a simple idea. Security should help a business move forward, not slow it down.

Many growing companies know they need stronger security. Few have the internal team or specialist expertise to build it themselves. Oragrol exists to close that gap. We start by understanding the business, then find the risks that actually matter, then give a clear and practical path to fixing them.

We are starting in Canada and expanding across North America from here.

---

## 3. Team

**Founder Led, Built to Scale**

Oragrol is founder led today. We work with specialist technology and delivery partners where it makes sense, rather than building out a large team before there is a real reason to.

## 4. Values / Differentiators

**Our Difference**

**Business First**
Security decisions should support how a business actually runs, not sit apart from it.

**Practical, Not Complicated**
Clear priorities and recommendations a team can actually act on.

**Built for What's Next**
Cybersecurity is where Oragrol starts, not where it stops. As the company grows, that same practical approach will carry into whatever the next important problem turns out to be.

**One-line summary:**
Oragrol combines cybersecurity expertise and business thinking to help organizations act on what matters now, and as the company grows.
