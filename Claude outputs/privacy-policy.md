# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 4, 2026
**Last updated:** September 4, 2026

## In plain language

We collect your name, business contact details, and information you give us through our contact form, live chat, and Cyber Health Score assessment so we can respond to you, prepare proposals, and deliver the results you asked for. We don't sell your personal information. We use a small number of trusted service providers to run our website, CRM, and email — some of them store data outside Canada, which is disclosed below. You can ask us what we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time.

## 1. Who we are

ORAGROL Global ("ORAGROL," "we," "us," or "our") is a Canadian managed security services provider. Our registered headquarters is in Thunder Bay, Ontario, with a business presence in Toronto, Ontario. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website at orgro.ca (the "Site") and through our related business communications with you, including live chat, our contact and enquiry forms, and our Cyber Health Score assessment tool.

This policy is written to comply with the *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA), the federal private-sector privacy law that applies to ORAGROL's collection, use, and disclosure of personal information in the course of commercial activity in Canada.

## 2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal information we collect:

- directly from you, when you fill out a form, start a chat conversation, complete the Cyber Health Score assessment, or otherwise contact us; and
- automatically, through standard website technology, when you browse the Site.

It does not apply to information you provide to us once you become a client and a separate signed services agreement is in place — that relationship is governed by the terms of your contract, which may include additional confidentiality and data-handling obligations specific to the security work we perform for you.

## 3. What personal information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

- **Contact details** — name, business email address, company name, job title, and phone number if you choose to provide one.
- **Enquiry and scoping information** — the message content, service interests, and project notes you submit through our contact form, our "My Scope" tool, or live chat.
- **Cyber Health Score assessment data** — your responses to our security-posture questionnaire, including general information about your industry, employee count, and the technology platforms your business uses (for example, whether you run Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace). We do not ask for, and you should not submit, passwords, account credentials, or any information that would grant us access to your systems through this tool.
- **Technical and usage information** — standard web log data such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and pages visited, collected automatically as part of operating the Site.

We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and, where required by law, obtain your consent through a cookie notice before any non-essential cookie is set.

## 4. How we use your information

We use the personal information we collect to:

- respond to your enquiry and prepare a proposal or quote;
- generate and deliver your Cyber Health Score results and follow up about them;
- create and maintain a record of our business relationship with you in our customer relationship management (CRM) system;
- operate, secure, and improve the Site and our live chat service;
- meet our own legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations; and
- communicate with you about your enquiry or, where you have consented, about our services.

We do not use your personal information for any purpose materially different from the one it was collected for without first obtaining your consent.

## 5. Our legal basis for collecting your information

Under PIPEDA, we rely on your knowledge and consent to collect, use, and disclose personal information, except where the law permits collection without consent (for example, to comply with a legal obligation). Your consent may be express — such as ticking a checkbox on a form — or implied by the fact that you have voluntarily provided information for an obvious purpose, such as submitting a contact form to receive a reply. You may withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice, by contacting us as described in Section 15.

Where we send you marketing or promotional email, we do so in compliance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), including honouring unsubscribe requests promptly and identifying ourselves clearly in every message.

## 6. Who we share your information with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We share personal information with a small number of service providers who process it on our behalf, strictly to help us operate our business:

- **HubSpot** — customer relationship management (CRM) and lead tracking.
- **Brevo** — transactional and marketing email delivery.
- **Vercel** — website hosting and infrastructure.
- **APITemplate.io** — generation of your Cyber Health Score report document.
- **n8n** — internal workflow automation connecting the tools above.

Each of these providers is contractually and technically restricted to using your information only to provide their service to us, and not for their own independent purposes. We may also disclose personal information where required by law, such as in response to a valid court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ORAGROL, our clients, or others.

## 7. Cross-border storage of your information

Some of the service providers listed in Section 6 store or process data on servers located outside Canada, including in the United States. This means your personal information may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions, including lawful access requests by foreign authorities. By submitting information to us, you acknowledge this cross-border processing. We select service providers who maintain industry-standard security and privacy commitments, and we limit what we share with each provider to what is reasonably necessary for the purpose described above.

## 8. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, to maintain our business records, and to meet legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements. Cyber Health Score assessment responses and enquiry records are generally retained for the duration of our prospective or active business relationship with you, plus a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping purposes. You may request earlier deletion at any time, subject to Section 9.

## 9. Your privacy rights

Subject to certain legal exceptions, you have the right to:

- ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you and to access a copy of it;
- ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
- withdraw your consent to our collection, use, or disclosure of your information; and
- ask us to delete personal information we no longer need for the purposes it was collected for.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 15. We will respond within the timelines required by PIPEDA and may ask you to verify your identity before acting on your request.

## 10. How we protect your information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we hold, including restricted access to systems containing personal information and secure, encrypted connections for data submitted through the Site. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach involving your personal information that creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required by law.

## 11. Children's privacy

Our Site and services are directed at businesses and business professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, our service providers, or the law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated on this page.

## 13. How to contact us

For any question about this policy or to exercise your privacy rights, contact us through the form on our [Contact page](/contact). A dedicated privacy mailbox will be published here once it is active.

Our registered headquarters: Thunder Bay, Ontario, Canada.
Our business presence: Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

## 14. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC), the federal regulator responsible for PIPEDA compliance, at priv.gc.ca.
