# Accessibility Statement

**Effective date:** September 4, 2026
**Last updated:** September 4, 2026

## In plain language

We want everyone, including people using assistive technology, to be able to use this website and get value from it. We're building it with accessibility in mind from the start, we're not yet claiming full formal certification, and we want to hear from you directly if something doesn't work — we'll fix it.

## 1. Our commitment

ORAGROL Global is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of ability or the technology used to access it. Accessibility is treated as an ongoing responsibility, not a one-time task, and is considered as our Site continues to evolve.

## 2. Standards we follow

As a business operating in Ontario, we design this Site with reference to Ontario's *Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005* (AODA) and its Integrated Accessibility Standards Regulation, which sets the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 Level AA as the applicable benchmark for organizational websites. Our internal target is the more current WCAG 2.1 Level AA standard, which builds on and includes the 2.0 AA requirements.

We have not yet completed a formal third-party accessibility audit of the Site, so we do not represent that the Site fully conforms to WCAG 2.1 AA at this time. This statement describes our current practices and our ongoing commitment, not a certified conformance claim.

## 3. What we build in

Where practical, we work to build our Site with:

- semantic HTML structure to support screen readers and other assistive technology;
- keyboard-navigable interface elements, including menus, forms, and interactive tools such as the Cyber Health Score assessment;
- meaningful alternative text for informative images;
- colour choices selected with contrast in mind between text and background; and
- clear, plain-language content, consistent with our broader brand approach of avoiding unnecessary jargon.

## 4. Known limitations and ongoing work

Our Site was substantially redesigned in 2026, and accessibility review of the new design is an active, ongoing process rather than a completed one. Some areas — including newer interactive tools and recently added pages — may not yet fully meet our WCAG 2.1 AA target. We are actively working through these as part of our regular development process, and we prioritize fixes based on their impact on real users.

## 5. Business size note

Ontario's Integrated Accessibility Standards Regulation applies different requirements based on organization size. ORAGROL Global currently operates with fewer than 50 employees. As a result, certain obligations that apply to larger organizations — such as maintaining a formal multi-year accessibility plan — are not yet legally required for us, though we intend to formalize our accessibility practices as the business grows.

## 6. Tell us about an accessibility barrier

If you encounter any difficulty using this Site, or if content or a feature is not accessible to you, we want to know. Please describe the issue, the page you were on, and the assistive technology or browser you were using (if applicable), through the form on our [Contact page](/contact). We will do our best to respond promptly and address the issue.

## 7. Alternative formats

If you need information from this Site in an alternative format, contact us through the [Contact page](/contact) and we will work with you to provide it in a way that works for you.

## 8. Changes to this statement

We will review and update this statement as our Site and our accessibility practices evolve. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
